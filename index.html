How to play
  • Arcade controls (default): drag or tap where you want to go, or use ← → (A / D). The boat moves across the current and keeps itself straight. ↑ / W = faster, ↓ / S = slower. ⚡ BOOST (E or Shift) punches through holes. Fill the boost meter with clean lines, close calls, waves and dry bags.
  • Pro controls: the real oar model. You aim the boat yourself. Choose it in the menu or on the pause screen.
  • Collect and upgrade: grab 🎒 dry bags and ⭐ gold stars (gold sits next to danger). Spend River Bucks in the 🛠 Boat shop on Handling, Power and Hull. Finish the 3 daily goals for more Bucks.
  • Click or tap to go (Pro): click or tap a spot on the river and your boat rows there. Drag to steer. Right-click to back-row (pull) there. Double-click and hold to square up.
  • Keyboard: ← → or A / D turn · ↑ or W push (row forward) · ↓ or S pull (back-row) · Space / P / Esc pause.
  • Oars: pushing moves you forward. Pulling (back-rowing) is stronger, because you use your legs and back. One oar pushing while the other pulls spins the boat in place.
  • Fast-forward: ⏩ button or F key in calm water. It turns off by itself before rapids, camps and markers.
  • Phone: ⟲ ⟳ to turn, PUSH and PULL to row.
  • Ferry: angle the boat and PULL to move across the current. This is the real oarsman move.
  • Square up: hit waves and holes straight (bow or stern first). The ring around your boat turns green when your angle is safe.
  • Read the water: a smooth V pointing downstream is the safe tongue. A V pointing upstream has a rock at its tip. Holes have a white pile and a dark edge. Pour-overs have a smooth hump before the hole. Boils push water out and spin you.
  • Eddies: water behind rocks, on the inside of bends and at camp beaches flows upstream. Crossing an eddy line spins the boat.
  • Man overboard: big hits can throw a crew member out. Row to the orange swimmer to pull them back in.
  • 3D mode: near big rapids, landmarks and bridges the view goes 3D and you must really paddle. Raft: assisted. Kayak: alternate L / R strokes. Dory: L / R oar to pivot, BOTH for power, BACK to pull away. Tube: kick.
  • Stops: land at optional stops (🥾 hike, 🏊 swim, 🥪 lunch, 👀 look) for points and history.
  • Take-out: land at the take-out ramp at the end. If you miss it, the trip fails.
  • Stars and streaks: a clean line gives 2 stars, a clean and straight entry gives 3. Clean rapids in a row multiply your points. Your best run comes back as a ghost boat.
  • Overnight / Expedition: land at a camp by pulling into the eddy and touching the beach. In Expedition, manage food, water, health and morale.
Rapid names, order, camps and history follow the real rivers. Miles are close but some are approximate. Rapid layouts are game versions, not a river guide. Always scout with a real guidebook and get your permits.
